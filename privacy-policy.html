2 Player Games - Play Offline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the “2 Player Games - Play Offline” app (the “Game”) handles, and why.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Game handles information when you install or use it. It applies to the Game available on Google Play. The Game is developed and published by the team behind the email address in section 11, based in India. We handle information in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

2. Summary

  • The Game works offline and does not require an account, login or sign-up.
  • Your game data, settings, player names, scores and progress stay on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the Game is used and to fix crashes.
  • The Game does not show advertisements at this time, and we do not sell your information.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, files, precise location or any other information that identifies you personally. The Game has no chat, online multiplayer or social features.

4. Information stored on your device

The Game stores the following in local storage on your device:

  • Settings, such as sound, vibration, language and bot difficulty
  • Player names you type into the Game
  • Best scores and game progress

This information is not sent to us or to any other party. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

The Game uses the Firebase Core, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google. They collect the following information:

Analytics events
Custom events logged by the Game, such as starting or finishing a game, the game mode, bot difficulty, changed settings and session duration, along with standard Firebase events such as first open and session start.
App instance and device identifiers
A Firebase app instance identifier, which links events and crash reports from the same installation. Where available on the device, an Android advertising ID or OAID is also used. These identifiers are not linked to your name or other personal details by us.
Crash reports
Crash and non-fatal error reports, including stack traces, custom keys and breadcrumb logs of recent in-game actions, together with device model, operating system version and app version.
Approximate region
Firebase derives an approximate region from the IP address of the connection. We receive only aggregated reports and do not receive your precise location.

Analytics events are batched and sent when a network connection is available, so they may reach Firebase after they occurred. Player names are never included in analytics events or crash reports.

6. How we use information

We use the information described in section 5 only to:

  • Find, diagnose and fix crashes and errors
  • Understand which games and features are used, so we can improve the Game
  • Keep the Game working across different devices and Android versions

We do not use this information for targeted advertising, to build advertising or marketing profiles, or to contact you.

7. Third-party services

We rely on the following third parties:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Core, Analytics and Crashlytics), which processes analytics and crash data on our behalf under Google’s terms and privacy policy. You can read Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play services, which provides the advertising ID on devices that support it.
  • An open-source library that reads the device identifier on the device. It does not send information to us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Advertising

The Game does not show advertisements at this time. If we add advertising in future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so, and we will describe the advertising network and the data it uses.

9. Children

The Game is suitable for ages 3 and above. We do not ask children for personal information, and a child can play every part of the Game without providing any. We do not show targeted advertising to children. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children’s use of the Game and of their device.

10. Security and international transfers

Information sent to Google Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access to our Firebase project is limited to authorised people. Google may process analytics and crash data on servers located outside India, as permitted by law.

11. Your rights and contact

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or erase it. Because we do not collect your name or contact details, please email us from the address you wish to use, and include your device model and app instance identifier if you have them, so that we can locate the relevant information. You can also stop all analytics collection by uninstalling the Game.
