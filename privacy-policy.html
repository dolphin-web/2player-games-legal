Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Game handles and why.

1. Introduction

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you install or play “2 Player Games” (the “Game”). We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

2. About the Game

The Game is free and works offline. It has no account or login, no chat or online play, no in-app purchases and no advertisements. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

3. Data we collect

Gameplay events
Games opened, finished or left, game mode, bot level, play time and settings changed. Used to understand how the Game is played and to improve it.
Device identifier
An identifier assigned to the device (OAID or advertising ID). Used to link events and crash reports from the same device, not to identify you.
Crash reports
Error details, device model, operating system version and app version. Used to find and fix problems.
Data kept on your device
Settings, player names, best scores and progress. Never sent to us or anyone else, and erased when you uninstall the Game.

4. Who handles the data

We use Google Firebase to process the data described above on our behalf. Google may store this data on servers outside India, as the DPDP Act permits. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. How we use data

We use the data only to fix errors and crashes, to understand which games are played so we can improve them, and to keep the Game working across devices. We do not use it for targeted advertising or to build marketing profiles.

6. Children

The Game is suitable for ages 3 and above. We do not ask children for personal information, and a child can play every part of the Game without providing any. We show no targeted advertising to children.

7. Your rights

You, or a parent or guardian on behalf of a child, may ask us to tell you what data we hold or to erase it. Email us with your device model and device identifier so we can locate the relevant data.

8. Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit, and access to our Firebase project is limited to authorised personnel. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will inform you in the Game of any material changes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email us at info@dolphinwebsolution.com
