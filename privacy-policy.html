2 Player Games - Play Offline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how “2 Player Games - Play Offline” (the “App”) handles information. The App is a free, offline game. It has no user accounts, no login and no sign-up. Most information stays on your device. The App also uses Google Firebase to collect limited analytics and crash reports. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

2. Information we collect

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following in its local storage on your device: game settings (such as sound, vibration and bot difficulty), player names you type, best scores and game progress. This information is not sent to us or to any other party. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Information sent to Firebase

  • Analytics events: custom events logged through Firebase Analytics, such as game start, game finish, game mode, bot difficulty, settings changed and session duration. Firebase also records automatic events such as first open and session start.
  • Crash and error reports: crash reports, non-fatal exception logs, stack traces, custom keys and breadcrumb logs of recent in-game actions, collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and device state such as memory and storage.
  • Device identifier: an identifier assigned to the device (OAID or advertising ID), used to link analytics events and crash reports from the same device. It is not used to identify you by name.
  • Approximate region: derived from your IP address by Firebase. We do not receive your precise location.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photographs, videos, contacts, precise location, or payment information. The App does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Player names are never included in analytics events or crash reports.

3. How we use information

We use the information described in section 2 only to:

  • understand how the App is played, so that we can improve it;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors; and
  • keep the App working across different devices and operating system versions.

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing profiles or any purpose unrelated to the App.

4. How analytics and crash reporting work

The App includes the Firebase Core, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics software development kits (SDKs). These record analytics events and crash reports on your device and send them to Firebase. Events are batched and uploaded when a network connection is available, so a report may reach Firebase some time after the event occurred. The App works fully offline; analytics and crash data are sent only when you are connected.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services and components. Each handles data under its own terms, in addition to this policy.

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics): receives the analytics and crash information described in section 2. Data may be stored on Google’s servers, which may be located outside India.
  • Google Play services: provides the advertising identifier on devices that support it. The App reads this identifier and passes it to Firebase as the device identifier.
  • Open-source OAID library (Android_CN_OAID): reads the device identifier on the device. On some devices it queries the identifier service installed by the device manufacturer. To our knowledge, the library does not send this data to the manufacturer or to us.

6. Advertising

The App does not show advertisements at present and does not include any advertising SDK. If this changes, we will update this policy and inform you in the App before any advertising is introduced.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the service providers listed in section 5, to operate analytics and crash reporting, and where the law requires us to disclose it.

8. Children

The App is suitable for ages 3 and above. We do not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and the App can be played without providing any. We do not show targeted advertising to children.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • delete all information stored on your device by uninstalling the App;
  • stop analytics and crash reporting by uninstalling the App, or by turning off the relevant settings on your device where available; and
  • ask us for information about, or the deletion of, analytics or crash data we hold about your device. Email us with your device model and device identifier so that we can locate it.

A parent or guardian may make these requests on behalf of a child.

10. Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Access to our Firebase project is limited to authorised personnel. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above and inform you in the App of any material change.

12. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India.
